# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

## Overview

Watch Party Manager (WASH) is an open source Discord bot maintained by **TehKarmah**. WASH is designed to help Discord communities manage movie and TV show suggestions, voting, watch history, and related administrative tasks.

This Privacy Policy explains what information WASH stores, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

## Privacy by Design

WASH is designed with privacy as a core principle.

- WASH is self-hosted and runs on infrastructure controlled by the server owner.
- WASH minimizes the information it stores.
- WASH does not collect analytics or telemetry.
- WASH does not display advertising.
- WASH does not sell, rent, or share user data.
- WASH does not transmit your server's data to the developer.
- WASH does not send your server's data to any third-party AI service.

## Information WASH Stores

Depending on the features enabled by the server owner, WASH may store:

### Discord Identifiers

- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs
- Discord Channel IDs
- Discord Thread IDs
- Discord Role IDs
- Discord Message IDs

These identifiers are used to provide WASH's functionality and are not used for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

### Suggestion Data

WASH may store information related to submitted watch suggestions, including:

- Movie or television title
- IMDb metadata (where available)
- Genres
- Runtime
- Release year
- Suggestion status
- The Discord User ID of the original suggester
- Watch history
- Voting history related to the suggestion

### Configuration Data

WASH stores configuration chosen by the server administrators, including items such as:

- Collections
- Voting defaults
- Nominee selection settings
- Reminder settings
- "I Won't Watch" settings
- Archive destinations
- Administrative configuration

### Backup Files

If the server owner creates backups, WASH stores the backup files only where the server owner chooses to save them.

## Information WASH Does Not Collect

WASH does **not**:

- collect analytics
- collect telemetry
- profile users
- track users across servers
- monitor browsing activity
- collect advertising identifiers
- sell or share personal information

## Third-Party Services

WASH may retrieve publicly available movie or television metadata from supported services, such as IMDb, when suggestions are added.

WASH does **not** transmit your server's stored data to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or any other AI service.

## Data Storage

All WASH data is stored by the server owner on the system where WASH is hosted.

The maintainer, **TehKarmah**, does not receive copies of your server's data and has no remote access to your installation.

## Data Retention

Data remains stored until it is:

- deleted by a server administrator,
- removed through WASH's management commands,
- deleted by restoring a different backup, or
- removed by uninstalling WASH and deleting its data files.

The server owner controls all retained data.

## Your Rights

Because WASH is self-hosted, requests regarding stored data should be directed to the administrator of the Discord server where WASH is installed.

Server administrators control:

- data retention,
- backups,
- deletion,
- restoration, and
- removal of WASH from their server.

## Children's Privacy

WASH relies on Discord's own age requirements and is not intended to collect information directly from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as WASH evolves.

Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
